# Global Youth Finance Hackathon (GYFH) 2027

> **Free. Virtual. Global. For student builders.**  
> **Student-founded, industry-advised.**

- **Target Event Dates**: Friday, March 26 – Sunday, March 28, 2027 (48-hour global sprint)
- **Timeline**: 24-Week Master Plan (Kickoff: Week of October 19, 2026)
- **Target Reach**: 150–300 verified student registrants, 30–60 submitted projects, 12–20 mentors/judges, 3 in-kind sponsors.

---

## 🧭 The 3 Tracks (Sandbox & Testnet Only)

1. **Financial Literacy & Money-Management Games**
   - Gamified personal finance, interactive simulators, inflation/budget visualizers for Gen Z & Gen Alpha.
2. **Micro-Saving & Youth Banking Tech**
   - Automated savings jars, teen allowance tools, goal-oriented micro-investing visualizers.
3. **Social Finance & Decentralized Tech**
   - Transparent donation tracking, group savings circles (tontines/chit funds), community treasuries, verifiable credentials.  
   *(Strictly testnet/sandbox: No real funds, no token speculation, no live crypto).*
