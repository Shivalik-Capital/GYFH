# GYFH Judging Rubric & Competition Rules

## ⚖️ Evaluation Framework (100 Points Total)

| Criterion | Points | What Earns Top Scores (9–10 Band) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Technical Execution** | **25** | A working core flow end-to-end; clean, readable code; genuine API/sandbox integration; no mock data hard-coded to fake interactivity. |
| **Business & Financial Viability** | **25** | Clear youth user problem solved; plausible sustainability or monetization model; realistic scope for an MVP. |
| **UX/UI & Youth Fit** | **20** | First-time user reaches value in < 60 seconds; clean mobile-first or responsive design; accessible and intuitive for Gen Z/Alpha. |
| **Compliance & Responsible Design** | **15** | Demonstrates awareness of data privacy, age restrictions (COPPA/GDPR-K), risk disclosures; avoids predatory gamification or deceptive dark patterns. |
| **Innovation & Track Fit** | **10** | Fresh perspective on youth personal finance; solves an authentic problem rather than rehashing a generic budget calculator. |
| **Demo & Communication** | **5** | Concise 2–3 minute video pitch articulating problem, solution, architecture, and live demo clearly. |

---

## 🛡️ Core Competition Rules & Minor Safety

1. **Strictly Sandbox & Testnet Only**:
   - Zero real money transactions, zero live bank credentials, zero mainnet cryptocurrency.
   - Any project attempting to connect to live bank accounts or mainnet wallets will be automatically disqualified.
2. **Team Structure**:
   - Teams of 1–4 students. Solo entries are welcome.
   - Cross-school, cross-country, and mixed-skill teams are actively encouraged.
3. **Intellectual Property**:
   - Teams retain 100% of their intellectual property and code.
   - All code submitted must be open source during judging (public GitHub/GitLab repository).
4. **Original Work**:
   - Code must be built within the 48-hour event window (March 26–28, 2027).
   - Use of open-source libraries, frameworks, and AI tools (Claude, Gemini, Copilot) is permitted, but teams must disclose tool usage and explain what they personally engineered.
5. **Minor Protection Policy**:
   - Participants under 18 require parental/guardian consent at registration.
   - Mentors and adult judges may never direct message (DM) minors one-on-one; all office hours and reviews take place in public group channels or supervised stages.
