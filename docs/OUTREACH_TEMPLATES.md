# Outreach Email Templates

> **Core Philosophy**:  
> Keep it short (under 120 words), make the time commitment explicit (e.g. 2 hours max), highlight your identity honestly (*student-founded, industry-advised*), and include a single clear call-to-action.

---

## 👔 Template A: Judges & Mentors (Fintech Engineers & PMs)

**Subject**: 2 hours to judge a student fintech hackathon (fully remote)

```text
Hi [First Name],

I’m [Your Name], a high school student in [City, Country] and founder of the Global Youth Finance Hackathon (GYFH) — a free, virtual 48-hour event on March 26–28, 2027 for student builders across 15+ countries.

Our teams are building youth-focused fintech: money-literacy games, micro-saving apps, and social finance tools (all strictly in sandbox/testnet environments). 

I loved your recent [talk / article / launch of X], and your perspective on [specific topic, e.g., real-time payments / financial UX / compliance] would be invaluable for our students.

The commitment is small:
• One 60-minute live mentor session / Q&A, OR
• ~60 minutes asynchronously scoring 4–5 short video demos against our published 100-point rubric.

Would you be open to judging or mentoring? A quick "yes" and I'll send over the 1-page brief. If you’re not the right person, could you point me to a colleague?

Thank you,
[Your Name]
Student Organizer, Global Youth Finance Hackathon
[LinkedIn URL] | [Landing Site URL]
```

## 🤝 Template B: In-Kind Sponsors & API Partners (Sandbox/Dev Tools)

**Subject**: Student fintech hackathon: Partnering with [Company Name] (non-cash)

```text
Hi [First Name / Team],

I’m [Your Name], a high school student developer organizing the Global Youth Finance Hackathon (GYFH) on March 26–28, 2027: a free, virtual hackathon bringing together 200+ global student builders across 3 fintech tracks.

We are not asking for financial sponsorship. Instead, we are looking for in-kind support: [e.g., student API sandbox tier / cloud/IDE credits for teams / a 20-minute technical workshop / swag for category winners].

In return, [Company Name] gets:
1. Brand visibility to 200+ motivated Gen Z developers building functional prototypes.
2. A dedicated 20-minute workshop / tech talk slot during our pre-event prep week.
3. Post-event developer feedback and project showcase featuring [Company Name] integration.

Could I share our 1-page partnership brief with you, or is there a better contact for developer relations?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Founder, Global Youth Finance Hackathon
[Landing Site URL]
```
