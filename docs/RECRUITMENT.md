# Team Recruitment & Interview Playbook

> **Target Team Size**: 3–4 High School / Early College Organizers  
> **Rule of Thumb**: Recruit for reliability and finished deliverables, not titles. Avoid recruiting only close friends.

---

## 📢 Ready-to-Post Recruitment Blurb

### Variant A: For Hack Club / Student Discord Dev Servers
```text
Hey everyone! 👋 

I'm organizing the Global Youth Finance Hackathon (GYFH) — a free, virtual 48-hour global hackathon on March 26–28, 2027 where student developers build next-gen youth fintech, money-literacy games, and social finance tools (strictly in sandbox/testnet environments).

We're aiming for 200+ global student builders and industry judges from top tech & fintech companies.

I'm looking for 2-3 driven student co-organizers to lead core functions:
1. Tech & Operations Lead (Discord architecture, registration flows, starter kit repos)
2. Marketing & Outreach Lead (Ambassador program across 15+ countries, developer communities)
3. Community & Mentors Lead (Mentor onboarding, workshop coordination, participant experience)

Commitment: ~3–5 hours/week now, 8–10 hours closer to event time. 
If you like shipping real projects and want to build a marquee global event from scratch, check it out & apply here: [INSERT_FORM_LINK]

DM me or reply if you have questions!
```

---

## 📋 3-Step Vetting Process

1. **Step 1: Short Application (Google Form / Tally)**
   - Name, Age, School/City/Country, Timezone.
   - Link to 1 thing you built, designed, or organized (GitHub, live site, club event, writing).
   - Which role are you applying for?
   - What's your realistic weekly availability between now and April?

2. **Step 2: 100% Async Scenario Test (Over Discord text / Email)**
   *Zero calls required. Send them one real-world operational scenario via text:*
   - **Tech & Ops**: *"How would you structure Discord channels and permissions for 300 teenagers who have never met before, to make sure they actually form teams without spam?"*
   - **Marketing & Outreach**: *"Draft the first 3 sentences of a cold message to a high school coding club president in Singapore or California asking them to share GYFH."*
   - **Community & Mentors**: *"A mentor from a fintech company hasn't responded to their calendar invite 24 hours before their workshop. What exact message do you send them?"*
   *(Evaluate how clearly, quickly, and professionally they write. Remote hackathons run on written communication, not phone calls).*

3. **Step 3: The 2-Week Micro-Trial**
   Give them 1 real deliverable with a clear 7-day deadline:
   - *Tech*: Build a clean Discord template or set up the GitHub organization.
   - *Marketing*: Create a list of 25 active high school CS clubs with contact links.
   - *Community*: Draft the 1-page Code of Conduct or Mentor Handbook draft.
   *(Confirm full role ownership only after they ship this deliverable).*
