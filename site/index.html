Scoring Criteria

The 100-Point Evaluation Spec

25%
Technical Execution
A working core flow end-to-end; clean git commits; genuine API or smart contract integrations; zero hard-coded fake data.
25%
Financial Viability
A defined youth pain point; plausible unit economics; thoughtful distribution plan; realistic MVP scope.
20%
UX & Youth Ergonomics
First-time user discovers value in under 60 seconds. Accessible, engaging, and free of predatory dark patterns.
15%
Compliance & Safety
Respects minor data privacy (COPPA/GDPR-K), risk disclosures, and avoids gamified gambling mechanics.
10%
Track Innovation
A fresh angle on personal finance rather than a generic expense tracker or loan calculator.
05%
Demo Video & Pitch
A 3-minute video pitch highlighting the user flow, codebase architecture, and live screen-share walkthrough.
Minor Protection

Zero Real Money & Supervised Mentorship

GYFH is purely educational. No real currency or live crypto is allowed. All adult mentors communicate exclusively in recorded public Discord stages or supervised queues.
